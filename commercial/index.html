Kitchen Climate Control

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and guests notice the drafts and the heat right away. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.
